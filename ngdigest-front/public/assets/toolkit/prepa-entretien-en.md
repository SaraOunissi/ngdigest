## My questions for them

> Ask at least three. They're what makes the end of an interview land.

- How does code review work, and who has the final say?
- What's the test coverage today, honestly?
- Over the last six months, how much time went to technical debt?
- What made the last person on the team leave?
- What does a successful first three months look like?
- Who decides prioritisation, and is front-end consulted before?
